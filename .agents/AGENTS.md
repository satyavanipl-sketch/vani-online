# VaniOnline Content-Generation Workflow Rules

These permanent rules apply to all NEW blog posts created on VaniOnline.com. Do not modify existing published posts.

## 1. Content Length
*   **Standard children's story:** Target 600 to 700 words.
*   **Simple preschool story:** 450 to 600 words is acceptable.
*   **Detailed moral, adventure, or educational story:** 700 to 900 words is acceptable only when the story genuinely needs more depth.
*   *Note:* Quality, readability, and usefulness are more important than an exact word count. Do not artificially increase the word count with repetition, filler, or unnecessary explanations.

## 2. Recommended Article Structure
Where appropriate, structure new stories as follows:
1.  **Introduction:** SEO-friendly hook, approximately 50 to 70 words.
2.  **Main Story:** Approximately 400 to 450 words.
3.  **Moral of the Story:** Summary of the core lesson, approximately 40 to 70 words.
4.  **What Children Learn:** Clear takeaways, approximately 30 to 50 words.
5.  **Interactive Questions:** 2 to 3 simple discussion questions for kids.
6.  **Read Next:** Section containing exactly 3 relevant internal links.

## 3. SEO Requirements
*   **Primary Focus Keyword:** Select one clear, story-specific focus keyword based on the character, moral theme, or age group. Do not use generic keywords like "bedtime story for kids" for every post.
    *   *Examples:* `kindness story for kids`, `honesty story for kids`, `sharing story for kids`, `respect story for kids`, `courage story for kids`, `forgiveness story for kids`, `animal story for kids`, `rabbit story for kids`, `elephant bedtime story for kids`, `turtle story for kids`, `calming bedtime story for kids`, `bedtime stories for kindergarten`, `moral story for kids`.
*   **Secondary Keywords:** Use 2 to 4 relevant secondary keywords naturally. No keyword stuffing.
*   **SEO Title:** Target 50 to 60 characters. Always preserve the original story name in the SEO title (e.g. `[Story Name] | [Focus Keyword]`).
*   **Meta Description:** Target 140 to 160 characters. Write a unique, natural description for every post. Avoid repetitive templates (such as *"Read the lovely bedtime story..."*).
*   **Search Console Signals:** Prioritize relevant query phrases identified in Google Search Console.

## 4. Internal Linking
*   Include **approximately 3 relevant internal links** to existing VaniOnline stories in the "Read Next" section.
*   Only link to genuinely related content:
    *   Kindness/Honesty story -> other kindness, honesty, or general moral stories.
    *   Animal/Kindergarten story -> related animal, bedtime, or age-appropriate stories.
*   Do not add irrelevant links just to increase counts.

## 5. Image Requirements
*   **Standard Count:** Generate exactly **6 images** by default:
    1.  Featured/cover image
    2.  Story scene 1
    3.  Story scene 2
    4.  Story scene 3
    5.  Story scene 4
    6.  Ending or moral scene
*   **Maximum Limit:** Maximum of 8 images per post. Only exceed 6 images when the story genuinely requires additional visual scenes. Avoid multiple images for repetitive scenes.
*   **Image Consistency:** Maintain strict visual consistency for character appearances, clothing styles, colors, art style, age characteristics, and setting environments across all images in a story.

## 6. Image SEO, Accessibility & Optimization
*   **SEO & ALT Tags:** Every image must have a descriptive, custom filename and meaningful ALT text incorporating the story topic naturally (no generic alt text like "image1" or keyword stuffing).
*   **Optimization:** Convert/compress all images to optimized WebP format with reasonable dimensions suitable for fast mobile page load speeds without sacrificing visual quality.

## 7. Readability & Vocabulary
*   Write for parents reading aloud to young children.
*   Use short paragraphs, short sentences, simple English, clear subheadings, and age-appropriate vocabulary.
*   Avoid long explanations, dry filler, or word-stuffing.

---

## 📋 Pre-Publishing Checklist
Before scheduling or publishing any post, verify:
- [ ] Word count is in the correct target range (450–700 words, up to 900 for detailed stories).
- [ ] Primary and secondary focus keywords are unique and naturally integrated.
- [ ] SEO title is 50–60 characters and preserves the story name.
- [ ] Meta description is a unique, natural description of 140–160 characters.
- [ ] Approximately 3 relevant internal links are included in a "Read Next" section.
- [ ] Image count is exactly 6 (maximum 8) and characters look visually consistent.
- [ ] Image ALT texts are meaningful and descriptive.
- [ ] Images are optimized as WebP and compressed for mobile viewing.
